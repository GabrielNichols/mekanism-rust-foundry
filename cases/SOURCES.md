# Pilot binding provenance

- Reference: Minecraft 1.21.1, NeoForge 21.1.200 and Mekanism 10.7.19.85. The Mekanism jar SHA-256 is `004dbc9f3106f4d192aeaa1ee1190dd16ec9ca8059ed3d093b80034f4c574f43`.
- The pinned jar's data recipe `data/mekanism/recipe/processing/iron/dust/from_ore.json` declares `mekanism:enriching`, one unit of `c:ores/iron` and two units of `mekanism:dust_iron`. The data JSON was read without examining the mod's Java implementation.
- NeoForge FE and item handlers are queried through public APIs. Initial discovery observed an 8,000 FE capacity and three unsided slots. NBT slot numbering was not assumed to define handler numbering.
- The first attempt to insert into slot 0 was rejected even though `isItemValid` returned true. The case now requires the result of `insertItem(..., simulate=true)` before actual insertion. A rejected mutation ends its attempt.
- Further discovery confirmed insertion through face `up`, slot 0, and extraction through face `west`, slot 0. The unsided handler remains a read witness: its simulations rejected the tested insertions/extractions. Complete slot records are retained; pilot totals are identified by item.
- `processing-03` observed world advancement without continued machine progress after 154. The next fixture declared and verified `forceload` for the target chunk. In `processing-04`, one ore produced two dust in 200 active ticks, consuming 4,000 FE; the saved world was checked independently.
- `lifecycle-01` observed three recipes, six naturally produced dust and total consumption of 12,000 FE. With a finite 100 FE supply, progress reached 5 when energy ran out and remained at 5 after five further ticks. `enrichment-lifecycle-v2` fixes these observations as explicit expectations for fresh repetition. It does not drain energy through a channel the reference does not permit.
- Administrative placement applies the item's default components and calls the public placement callback. It does not represent player ingress. The separate `client-placement-menu.json` case uses actual keybinding/Screen handling and checks server and client effects. `client-processing-menu.json` extends that path through production and output pickup.
- `fixture_block_output` requires a machine that has already produced exactly two actual dust. It explicitly changes that saved stack to 64 as administrative setup, reloads the entity and checks the public handler. It does not qualify player-driven filling/removal or the Configurator. Subsequent extraction uses the public handler declared in the case.
- Duration and consumption were not derived from future-port code. `advance_until` finds the result under an explicit budget, while detailed capture records the full trajectory. Observed results still need a sealed expected manifest and additional controls before authorizing Rust gameplay.

Exact attempts and hashes remain in private evidence directories. Recipe quantity expectations are not automatically updated after failures. A/A repetition and capture-frequency comparison do not establish Java/Rust equivalence.
